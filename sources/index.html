Photos

Fighter images are real photographs. Where a promotion publishes official fight-card photos of its fighters (for example UFC), we show those, credited to the promotion and linked to its event page; the rights belong to the promotion. Otherwise we use photographs from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses, matched to the fighter's own Wikipedia article and credited on the profile. Where neither exists we show a plain placeholder. We do not use AI-generated portraits. If you hold the rights to an image and want it removed, use the corrections page.
