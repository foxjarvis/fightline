About FIGHTLINE

Every card. Every fighter. Every combat sport.

FIGHTLINE is an independent combat-sports information platform built to make events, fighters, matchups, and results easier to find in one place.

We cover combat sports across multiple formats, including mixed martial arts, professional boxing, bare-knuckle boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai, and grappling. Our goal is to make it easier to discover upcoming events, explore announced fight cards, review available fighter records, compare competitors, and follow changes to the schedule.

What you can find here

  • Events: Upcoming cards, venues, dates, announced bouts, and available viewing information.
  • Fighters: Profiles, sourced records, available statistics, and upcoming matchups.
  • Matchups: Head-to-head information drawn from the data currently available to us.
  • Sources: References and verification notes showing where event and fighter information came from. See the source list.
  • Corrections: A way to report errors and request a review of published information. Report a correction.

Not every event has a complete card, and not every fighter has a complete profile. When information is missing or disputed, we aim to say so rather than fill the gap with a guess. We do not have a live scores feed.

Where our data comes from

We consult official promotion and event pages, governing-body publications, and other relevant sources for information about schedules, bouts, fighter records, and results. We retain source references and verification notes where available.

A published event may be confirmed in one respect, such as its date, while some of its individual bouts remain unconfirmed or disputed. We do not treat every detail on an event page as equally verified.

Our source notes and verification labels explain what we checked and any material uncertainty we identified. Information can change after publication, so a previously checked item may still require an update. How we verify data.

Independent by design

FIGHTLINE is an independent project. It is not an official website of the UFC, PFL, ONE Championship, BKFC, any boxing promotion, governing body, broadcaster, or athlete unless a specific relationship is expressly disclosed.

Promotion names, event names, fighter names, and other identifying marks belong to their respective owners. Their appearance on FIGHTLINE is intended to identify the events and people being covered; it does not, by itself, imply sponsorship, approval, or affiliation.

Photographs and other third-party materials remain subject to their respective rights and license terms. We document photo credits and the applicable license on each fighter page where we use a photograph, and we show a plain placeholder where we have no licensed photo.

Our standard

Accuracy matters more than appearing complete. We would rather identify a missing detail than publish an unsupported one. When reliable new information becomes available, we aim to update the relevant page and preserve a record of material corrections.

FIGHTLINE is a developing service. Its coverage, features, and available data may change as the project grows.
