Corrections policy

Help us keep the record accurate.

FIGHTLINE aims to publish useful, well-sourced combat-sports information. If you believe a date, venue, bout, fighter record, result, title, method, or other detail is incorrect, we welcome a correction report.

What to include

  • The URL of the FIGHTLINE page.
  • The specific fact you believe is incorrect.
  • The proposed correction, if known.
  • A link to an official announcement, governing-body record, promotion page, or other relevant evidence.
  • Any context that may help us understand a disagreement between sources.

Reports that identify a specific fact and provide supporting evidence are easier to assess than general reports that a page is wrong.

Report a correction

Open a report on our public tracker: github.com/foxjarvis/fightline/issues/new. Include the page address, what looks wrong, and a link to evidence. Reports are public, so please do not include personal details.

How we review reports

We compare the reported information with the available sources. Where possible, we prioritize primary sources relevant to the fact in question.

If sources disagree or the evidence is incomplete, we may leave the item marked as unresolved or under review while we assess it. We do not promise a particular resolution or response time. Submitting a correction does not automatically mean the proposed change will be accepted.

What happens when a correction is accepted

When a material correction is warranted, we update the affected information and keep a record of the change, including its reason. Changes appear in the correction history on the relevant event or fighter page.

For a published fight result, a correction may also require reversing and recalculating the records and win-method statistics of the fighters involved. Those dependent figures are updated consistently with the corrected result. If a result has been officially overturned, we distinguish the overturned result from a replacement result that has not yet been verified, and we do not silently erase the prior record of the correction.

Disputed or overturned results

Where credible sources conflict, we may mark a result as under review or temporarily withdraw a disputed result from current calculations when leaving it in place would mislead readers.

We aim to identify the uncertainty and update the page when sufficient evidence becomes available. We do not treat an unverified claim, rumor, or unsupported social post as conclusive proof of an official result.

Editorial independence

Correction decisions are based on the available evidence, not on whether a change benefits a fighter, promotion, broadcaster, sponsor, or other interested party.

FIGHTLINE's coverage of an athlete or event does not imply an endorsement or official relationship. We reserve the right to decline requests that are unsupported, outside the scope of our coverage, or inconsistent with the available evidence.

Limits of this policy

FIGHTLINE is a developing service, and review capacity may vary. We cannot guarantee that every report will receive an individual reply or that every third-party source will remain available. We aim to make corrections transparently, preserve the context needed to understand material changes, and avoid presenting uncertain information as established fact.
